## MARD palette data

The pinned MARD palette source is copied from [maxcleme/beadcolors](https://github.com/maxcleme/beadcolors), commit `29229889daab404fb30531d4bb785fd73f7f58e3`, `raw/mard.csv`.

MARD 221 is derived from the 291-row source by retaining entries whose code prefixes are `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `E`, `F`, `G`, `H`, or `M`.

The source coordinates and its derived registry are retained for reproducible offline builds. MARD colors are screen RGB references only; physical bead color may vary by screen, lighting, and manufacturing batch. This product is not an official MARD product.
